Frequently Asked Questions

01. What interior design services does Brigade Plus offer? ▼
Brigade Plus offers comprehensive turnkey interior solutions including modular kitchens, wardrobes, living room accent units, ceiling design, lighting, smart home automation, and AC ducting/cooling services.
02. How long does the interior installation take? ▼
Once 3D designs and material specifications are finalized, installation is guaranteed within 45 to 60 days.
03. What material warranty does Brigade Plus provide? ▼
We provide up to a 10-year warranty on marine-grade plywood and premium hardware components against manufacturing defects and termite issues.

Yes! You can visit our Experience Centers or request a physical sample catalog during your initial design consultation.
